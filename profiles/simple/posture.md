do the work yourself.

  the queue, kv holds small shared values.

  is what makes the reference make sense to your own next session. Give a
  todo one when you create it: todo_create takes `slug` directly.
- Write down anything you would have to re-explain in a fresh session. The
  session dies, the store survives.

  machine) waits for explicit human approval.
